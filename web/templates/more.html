How FoodAlgo works

FoodAlgo trains a random-forest model on your meal history and learns which food tends to be served for each weekday and meal. It then predicts the most likely foods (with confidence) for the date and meal you pick. Add at least {{ min_rows }} meals; a few weeks of history gives the best results.

Uploading a file

Upload a .csv or .xlsx file with these columns:

  • Day, Month, Year — numbers (or a single Date column as YYYY-MM-DD)
  • Time — breakfast, lunch or dinner
  • Food — any text

Download the sample file to see the format. Your history is kept in your browser only.
